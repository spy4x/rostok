How We Engage

Every engagement runs on the same five policies. They are not negotiable. They exist so the work ships on time, the budget holds, and the IP is yours from Day One.

🛡️

14-Day Mutual Alignment Guarantee

If within 14 days the engagement is not the right fit — communication style, technical direction, or pace — you get 100% back, no questions asked. We absorb the onboarding cost. The first two weeks are paid only if both sides commit to the full project.

⏱️

Async-First Execution

Maximum one weekly sync call. Everything else happens via text, recorded Loom videos, and pull requests. You do not need to clear your calendar. You do not need to attend standups. Decisions are documented, not lost in meetings.

🔑

Clean Handoff & Full IP Ownership

From Day One you own 100% of the source code, infrastructure keys, and documentation. No vendor lock-in. No proprietary frameworks. No hostage situations. If we disappear tomorrow, you keep everything and run it yourself.

🎯

Fixed-Price Milestone-Based Billing

The total project price is set on Day One. Payment is tied to milestone delivery, not hours logged. We absorb scope creep. We refund milestones that miss. The budget you approved is the budget you spend.

🔧

30-Day Code Warranty

After deployment, any bug traced to our code is fixed free of charge for 30 days. The warranty is explicit, written into the contract, and honoured without invoicing theatre. Past that, we offer monthly support retainers from $400.
